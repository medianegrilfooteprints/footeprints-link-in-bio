Since 1985

The FootePrints story began in 1985, when the Foote family saw the potential in a beautiful stretch of Negril beach. What started as a twelve-room haven for adventurous travellers is now a 32-room boutique hotel, still Jamaican-owned and still in the family.

Questions we get asked

Is FootePrints all-inclusive?

We’re a boutique hotel rather than an all-inclusive resort, and continental breakfast is included with every room.

If you’d like everything covered, add our optional All-Inclusive Plan: $165 per adult and $100 per child aged 4–12, per night, for the whole stay.

Rooms and the plan
How do room prices work, and can children stay?

Our rates are per room, not per person: from $259 a night for two adults, with continental breakfast, plus taxes and fees.

One child can share your room at no extra room charge. Children 0–3 stay completely free, and for ages 4–12 you only add continental breakfast, at $20 a night. A third adult (13 and over) is $25 a night, taxes included.

See the rooms
What should I know before I arrive?

Check-in is from 3 PM and check-out is by 11 AM.

Late check-out is $65, available when the hotel isn’t fully booked and your room isn’t needed for arriving guests: just ask the front desk.

We’re a cashless property, so please bring a card, and hotel guests have a $200 card pre-authorisation at check-in.

Is there a local rate for Jamaican residents?

Yes. Message reservations on WhatsApp for the current local rate.

WhatsApp reservations
Can I visit for the day or just for a meal?

Yes, you don’t need to stay with us. Walk in for lunch at Blushy’s or dinner at Gatsby’s (we recommend booking dinner).

Or spend the day with a Day Pass: $30 per adult, $20 for ages 4–12, free for ages 0–3. The pass includes the beach, the infinity pool, loungers, umbrellas, towels and a welcome drink.

Food and drinks are not included: you can order lunch and drinks from Blushy’s at an extra cost, and they’re brought to your lounger.

Please reserve the Day Pass ahead: spaces are limited, and on private event days the beach closes to day visitors.

Day Pass Dining and menus
Can we celebrate or get married with you?

We’d love that. Birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers and private dinners are all welcome.

For weddings, we host everything from an intimate vow renewal on the beach to a celebration for up to 100 guests with all 32 rooms booked for your party.

Weddings and celebrations
Can we bring a group, a retreat or our company?

Yes. Wellness retreats, workshops, team trips and family reunions are all welcome, and the whole hotel can be yours.

For meetings and conferences, your team has a professional event space: 3,300 sq ft, air-conditioned, with flexible layouts, two 75-inch screens, a projector, Wi-Fi, hybrid set-up and on-site tech support.

Retreats and groups
Is this the old Foote Prints on the Sands?

Yes. It is the same beachfront property on Seven Mile Beach, fully renovated and renamed Negril FootePrints Hotel.

Day Pass questions

Can I book a Day Pass for today?

Yes. Same-day bookings are welcome: reserve online, then come straight over.

Can I come without booking?

You can try, but we can’t promise you a spot. Day Pass spaces are limited each day, and the beach closes to day visitors on private event days, on fully booked days and on some holidays.

To be sure of your day, reserve online before you come.

What happens if it rains?

The Day Pass goes ahead rain or shine, and we don’t refund it for the weather once you’ve arrived.

Please check the forecast before you come. If it doesn’t look good, you can move your Day Pass to another date before your visit.

Is it cheaper if I arrive late or leave early?

No. The price is the same for the day, whether you arrive at 9 AM or 1 PM, and whether you leave at noon or at 5.

Can I book for a big group?

Yes, online like any other booking. You can add up to 10 people in one booking; for a bigger group, simply make a second booking for the same date.

Spaces are limited each day, so book early for a group.

If we eat at the restaurant, can we stay on the beach?

A meal at Blushy’s or Gatsby’s doesn’t include the beach or the pool.

To spend the day on the loungers, book a Day Pass, then order food and drinks to your chair.

Can we bring our own food and drinks?

Food and drinks are served by Blushy’s Bar & Grill, so please don’t bring your own.

Baby food and snacks for little ones are of course fine.

Do children need a ticket?

Yes, please add everyone to your booking: $20 for ages 4–12, and a free ticket for ages 0–3, so we know how many of you are coming.

Dining questions

Do I need to stay at the hotel to eat here?

No. Breakfast, lunch and dinner are open to everyone. Walk in for breakfast or lunch at Blushy’s, or book a table for dinner at Gatsby’s.

Do I need a reservation for dinner?

Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking dinner at Gatsby’s, and especially on holidays and special days.

Reserve at Gatsby's
Is there a dress code?

At Gatsby’s in the evening: resort casual. No swimwear, cover-ups or bare feet, please.

Do you have a children’s menu?

Children are very welcome. A children’s menu is coming soon, and high chairs are available. Until then, our team is happy to help you choose.

Is there a happy hour?

Yes, every day from 4 to 6 PM: 2-for-1 on selected cocktails and martinis, Red Stripe and Blushy’s Special.

See the drinks menu
How many people can you seat together?

Up to 10 guests at one table. For a bigger group, contact us and we’ll plan it with you.

What if I have a food allergy?

Please mention it when you book on Eat App, and tell your server when you order.

Do you have vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free dishes?

Yes. Dinner has a vegetarian section, and dishes are marked V (vegan) and G (gluten-free) on every menu.

Are tax and service included in the prices?

Menu prices are in US dollars and are subject to 10% government tax and 15% service charge.

How do I pay?

By card. FootePrints is a cashless property.

Questions

Can I apply as both a creator and a model?

Yes. Choose the path closest to you in the first question. At the end of the form you can ask us to consider you for both.

I don’t have hotel, restaurant or spa content yet. Should I still apply?

We’d suggest creating some first, then applying. Hospitality content is what we look at most, and without it we’re unlikely to be able to work together.

Do you provide outfits for shoots?

No. Models bring their own outfits, so please apply only if you have pieces that suit a relaxed, refined beachfront hotel.

Are collaborations paid?

It depends on the collaboration. The form asks how you’d like to work: complimentary, paid, or open to discussing. Everything is agreed in writing before you arrive.

Do you work with outside photographers or videographers?

No. We produce our own photography and video, so we are not taking on outside photographers or videographers.

When will I hear back?

We read every application personally. If there’s a good fit, we’ll reach out by email or DM.

Careers

FootePrints is a family-owned hotel on Seven Mile Beach, welcoming guests since 1985. We’re small, with 32 rooms, a spa and two restaurants, and that’s what guests love about us: they get to know the people who look after them.

What we look for

  • Warmth: you enjoy looking after people
  • Care for the details guests notice
  • Reliability: your team can count on you
  • Experience helps, and so does a good fit with our team

How we hire

  1. Apply online in a few minutes. No CV needed yet.
  2. When a role opens, we look through the applications.
  3. If your experience fits, we contact you for your CV and an interview.

We contact applicants only when a suitable role opens. If you don’t hear from us, we still have your details for future roles.

Questions

Do I need a CV to apply?

Not at this step. If you have a CV or LinkedIn online, you can add the link. If a role fits, we’ll ask for your full CV.

What kinds of roles are there?

Roles across the hotel: front desk and reservations, housekeeping, the kitchen, the restaurant and bar, the spa, and maintenance and grounds. Choose up to three areas in the form.

When will I hear back?

We contact applicants when a role opens that fits their experience. We can’t reply to every application, but we keep your details for future roles.

FootePrints is a boutique hotel, not an all-inclusive resort. Continental breakfast always comes with your room. The plan is an optional extra, for guests who’d like everything covered.

  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner

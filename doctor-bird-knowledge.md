## Answers

### Is FootePrints all-inclusive?
Topic: stay
Words: all inclusive plan meals drinks included food package
Links: Rooms and the plan = #stay

FootePrints is a **boutique hotel, not an all-inclusive resort**. **Continental breakfast always comes with your room.**

If you'd like everything covered, add our optional **All-Inclusive Plan**:
- **$165 per adult (13+)**, a night
- **$100 per child aged 4–12**, a night
- **Free** for children 3 and under

**What's included:** breakfast, lunch and dinner; water, soft drinks, juices, coffee and tea; beer, wine by the glass, cocktails and all spirits by the glass; and the minibar in your room, restocked once a day.

**Not included:** bottles of alcohol, spa treatments, airport transfers and excursions, and special experiences marked as extra.

**The rules:** the plan is for the **whole stay**, not for single days, and it is added for **everyone staying in the room**. Children 3 and under are free when sharing the room with two adults on the plan.

To add it, tick the All-Inclusive Plan in your room request: you'll see the total for your stay before you send it.

### How do room prices work, and can children stay?
Topic: stay
Links: See the rooms = #stay | Book online = https://negrilfooteprints.com/search-results/

Our rates are **per room, not per person**: from **$259 a night for two adults**, with continental breakfast. Prices change with the season.

- **One child** can share your room at no extra room charge.
- Children **0–3 stay free**.
- Children **4–12**: you only add continental breakfast, **$20 a night**.
- A **third adult (13 and over)** is **$25 a night**, taxes included.
- **Cribs** are available on request.

Our rooms are for 2 adults and 1 child. Travelling with more people? Tell us in your room request and we'll help.

### Can I visit for the day or just for a meal?
Topic: daypass
Words: day pass price cost ticket visitors non guests walk in
Links: Book a Day Pass = https://www.tickettailor.com/events/negrilfooteprintshotel/2452834 | Day Pass details = #daypass | Dining and menus = #dining

**Yes, you don't need to stay with us.** Walk in for breakfast or lunch at Blushy's, or dinner at Gatsby's (we recommend booking dinner).

Or spend the day with a **Day Pass**, daily **9 AM – 5 PM**:
- **$30** per adult, **$20** for ages 4–12, **free** for ages 0–3
- **Included:** a welcome drink, towels, the infinity pool, loungers and umbrellas, your spot on Seven Mile Beach, a lifeguard, an outdoor beach shower, restrooms by the beach, power outlets, complimentary kayaks, the FootePrints swing and board games.
- **Not included:** food and drinks. Order lunch and drinks from Blushy's at an extra cost, and they're brought to your lounger.

**How it works:** reserve online, get your QR code by email (nothing is charged online), arrive any time from 9 AM, show your QR code at the front desk and pay by card.

The Day Pass ends at 5 PM, but nobody will rush you: stay for the sunset and dinner at Gatsby's from 6 PM.
